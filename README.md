## 🔬 About Me

I am a veterinarian, researcher, and Assistant Professor at the University of São Paulo (FMVZ-USP), Brazil.

My research focuses on integrating veterinary oncology, endocrinology, and gastroenterology to investigate disease mechanisms, improve diagnostic strategies, and develop innovative therapeutic approaches.

I am particularly interested in translational research, molecular biomarkers, host–microbiome interactions, and precision veterinary medicine.
